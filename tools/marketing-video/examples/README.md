# Approved card style (owner 2026-11-07)

These layouts are cards the owner approved and published on 2026-11-07, rebuilt
for `make-card.mjs`. They show the style. They are not templates to copy: use
the same ingredients in a new composition every day.

- References the owner chose:
  - Sauna.ai: cream background, serif headline, tool chips dropped into a
    feature list.
  - Chase AI: laptop, floating chips, a checklist sticky note, an "in N steps"
    headline. Chase uses that as a carousel cover; our cards are posted one at
    a time, so a card shows all its steps and carries no page marker such as
    "1/5".
  - Runable: a physical object, such as a receipt or a ticket, that explains
    the idea at a glance.
- Rejected styles:
  - cards cut from dense screenshots;
  - one fixed layout with only the words changed;
  - lifestyle or furniture-shop photos;
  - anything only about Review.
- `every-ai.layout.html`: typography and chips only, no photo. Provenance is
  `operator_upload`.
- `answer-receipt.layout.html`: an AI-made receipt photo with exact words set on
  it, from a real Tomverse analysis (consensus 7, differences 6, missing points
  7, to verify 6, contradictions 1). It shows the AI line. Provenance is
  `ai_modified`.
- `assistant-steps.png`: the published card that used the AI-made laptop photo
  with floating model chips, a "Family trip planner" card and a four-step
  checklist.
